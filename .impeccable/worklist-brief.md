# Worklist surface

Mode: Operate. Editor in a bright hackathon hall or at a desk, comparing passages and opening exact revisions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An editorial desk with a ranked queue beside an open evidence document. The queue remains visible while reading a claim.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white paper, navy ink, blue selection and restrained amber review marks. Sans UI labels and readable serif quotations. Thin rules and broad document columns, with no nested cards.

STORY: Understand the editor's task, select an article, inspect a candidate alignment, distinguish coverage from a fact conflict, open the cited revision.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact top navigation, one sentence explaining the job, left ranked article list occupying one third, and the selected article with three language columns on the right. One review candidate already open.

FORM: Code-led editorial comparison desk selected under the user's autonomous build authorization. No pre-existing visual identity or approved comp. Seed: brief-derived, no random seed used.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
