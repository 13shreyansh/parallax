# Citation clarification after independent review

The frozen academy control’s source pointer quotes the introductory paragraph, which states the year 1640. The full date is in the same frozen source article’s History section. This clarification does not change the frozen facts, cases, source file or scores.

Source: [Royal Academy of Turku, revision 1280830549](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=1280830549); `source-articles.json`, English Q414481, History.

> The academy was founded on 26 March 1640 by Queen Christina of Sweden at the proposal of Count Per Brahe, on base of Åbo Cathedral School (founded 1276). It was the third university in the Swedish Empire, following Uppsala University (founded 1477) and the Academia Gustaviana (now the University of Tartu in Estonia) (1632).
